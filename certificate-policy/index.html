About earlier certificates.

Solvanta has retired its courses. This page explains what that means for certificates downloaded or issued earlier.

New certificates

We are not offering new courses, assessments or course certificates on the current Solvanta site.

Downloaded certificate images

Earlier assessment pages could create a downloadable certificate image directly in the visitor's browser. A downloaded image or randomly generated certificate ID does not by itself establish that a record was registered with the public verification database.

Checking a record

Use the existing certificate lookup to check whether an ID matches a stored record. If no record is found, it may mean that no matching record was ever created, that the ID is incorrect, or that a record is no longer available.

What the certificates mean

A Solvanta course certificate was for personal learning or assessment participation. It is not an accredited qualification, licence, government approval or guarantee of employment.

Questions or corrections

If you need help with an older certificate, contact us. You may also request correction or deletion of a stored record through our data deletion process.
