Certificate information.

For people who participated in Solvanta's earlier learning activities.

New certificates

Solvanta's former learning courses are not currently offered. We are not issuing new course certificates through this website at this time.

Previously downloaded certificates

Some previous assessments generated a downloadable certificate image directly in the browser. That does not guarantee that a matching record exists in the certificate verification system.

Public verification

If a certificate ID is present in our records, you can check its details on the certificate lookup page. If a record is missing, please contact us for help; we cannot guarantee that previously downloaded certificates can be verified.

Limitations

These certificates recognise personal learning activities only. They are not accredited qualifications, government approvals, licences, or guarantees of employment.

Privacy and corrections

For a correction or deletion request, use the contact page or read our data deletion information.
