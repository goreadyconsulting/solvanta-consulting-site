Privacy and your information

Your privacy matters.

Here's what happens to the information you enter, what we keep, and how to request deletion.

Solvanta information

We keep these pages clear so you know how the website works and how to contact us.

Last updated: 1 November 2026

The free tools

The message builder, text cleaner and percentage calculator run in your browser. Text and numbers you enter into those tools are processed on your device and are not sent to Solvanta's servers. You do not need an account to use them.

We do not intentionally add behavioural advertising or analytics tracking cookies to these tools. Your browser and hosting services may still process routine technical data needed to deliver the site.

When you contact us

If you use the contact form or email us, we receive the name, email address, enquiry category and message you provide. We use them to reply, investigate issues or consider tool suggestions. Contact information may be handled through Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Google Workspace or email services.

Earlier certificate records

Solvanta previously offered learning activities. Some certificate details may remain in the verification service, such as a certificate ID, learner name, course title, issue date, status and score where applicable. The public lookup is intended to show only the details needed to check a record—not a learner's email address or private contact information.

Some older downloadable certificate images were created entirely in the browser and may never have had a verification record.

How long information is kept

Contact enquiries are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond and manage related communication. Historical certificate records may be retained for verification or removed following a verified deletion request, subject to any legitimate retention needs.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information associated with an enquiry or older certificate record. Visit data deletion for the request process. Removing a certificate record can make it unavailable for public verification.

Services and security

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The contact and historical certificate features may use Google services. These providers may process technical or submitted data according to their respective policies. We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Children and changes

This site is not designed specifically for children. Users under 18 should obtain appropriate parental or guardian permission before submitting contact details. We may update this policy as the site changes, with the date shown here.

Contact

For privacy questions, email helpdesk@solvantaconsulting.com or send a message.
