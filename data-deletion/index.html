What the tools store

The free message, text and percentage tools do not send entered content to Solvanta or save it to a Solvanta account. There is normally no tool input record to request from us.

Contact messages

If you previously used our contact form or sent an email, you may request that we find and remove associated enquiry records, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Existing certificate records

You may request correction or deletion of a historical certificate record. Once a record has been deleted, its ID may no longer appear in the public lookup. A previously downloaded image is not proof that a database record exists.

How to request deletion

Send an email to helpdesk@solvantaconsulting.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. You can also use the contact page and choose “Something else”.

Tell us which information is involved and provide the minimum details needed to locate it—for example, the email address used for an enquiry or a certificate ID. We may need to confirm that you are authorised to make the request before changing another person's records.

What happens next

We will review the request and respond as soon as reasonably possible. We may ask for clarification or retain limited information where needed to handle the request, prevent misuse or meet applicable obligations.

Other questions
