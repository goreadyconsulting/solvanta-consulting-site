Useful by design.

Solvanta is a small project with a simple goal: make everyday work a little easier.

Less friction. More progress.

Many everyday tasks don't need another account, an expensive subscription or a complicated app. Sometimes you just need to write a clear message, tidy some text or check a number.

That's what Solvanta is here for: simple, free tools that work without getting in your way.

We're starting small and improving what we build based on actual needs—not adding features just for the sake of it.

Try the tools

Who's behind it?

Solvanta is a project by Omkar Reddy, who works across operations, process improvement and automation. The idea grew from a recurring question: “Can we make this easier?”

We want each tool to be understandable, respectful of your privacy, and helpful from the first click.
